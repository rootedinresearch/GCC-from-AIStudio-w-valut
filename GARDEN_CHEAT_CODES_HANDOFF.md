## 4. VERDICT FRAMEWORK

- ✅ **Supported** — Peer-reviewed research confirms The Old Ways
- ❌ **Myth / Unsupported** — Research contradicts or finds no effect
- ⚠️ **Partial / Inconclusive** — Evidence exists on both sides or is too limited
- 🔄 **Surprising Twist** — Partially right but for different reasons
- 🎓 **Extension Verified** — Anchored in land-grant extension office research
